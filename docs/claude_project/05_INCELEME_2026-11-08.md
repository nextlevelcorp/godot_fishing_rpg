# Game review, 2026-11-08 (builds 61 to 64)

Five read-only agents reviewed the game from the code on 2026-11-08: QA, architecture and web
performance, visual and UI, a benchmark against shipped fishing games, and retention and economy.
Nothing was run on a phone. The benchmark agent could not open store pages or wikis (the session's
network blocked them), so its competitor facts come from search-result text, with URLs, and are
marked as such. Every estimate below is labelled.

## Where the game stands (benchmark)

| Dimension | Standing | Note |
|---|---|---|
| First minute | Behind, now improved | Title, map, spot, walk, cast, then a wait of up to ~67 s. Build 63: first visit in 2.5-4.5 s for a new player, waits capped at 30 s. |
| Catch feel | Ahead (hypothesis) | Nibble vs bite, clutch, jumps, head shakes; Stardew-style games use one bar. Not confirmed on a phone. |

| Progression depth | Behind | 10 spots, 3 rigs, 1 net. |
| Collection | Behind | 26 shore species; 14 painted. |
| Social | Behind | Locals and rivals are simulated. |
| Realism | Ahead | Real legal sizes, seasons, moon, named winds, cited spots. |
| Content volume | Behind | 10 spots. |
| Reasons to return | Behind, now improved | Build 63: day-on-the-water bonus, summary shows today's challenge and the tournament spot. |

**Differentiators to protect:** real, cited Turkish shores tied to the real world (regulation, moon,
wind, season); realism readable in a short, fair session with no energy or gambling; a named local
at each spot.

## What was fixed (builds 62-64)

- **Bugs (QA):** tackle and test panels close once the line is out (hook and free-bait swaps
  mid-fight); MAP during a tournament asks before forfeiting the entry; the tackle panel scrolls
  with DONE at the top; a predator on a lure you keep winding hooks itself; a missed natural bait
  is used up; the tournament result closes other panels; ENTER only with the line in; the week's
  tournament spot is saved; double-tap GIVE ignored; saved bait stock clamped; CAST pins with REEL.
- **Performance (architecture):** crowd, trees, walkers and night glow culled to the camera's real
  width; glow redrawn only when the camera moves; nothing drawn under the first-person view and
  its glow layer hidden with it; HUD text at 4 Hz and only on change; sprites 1024 to 768 px
  (10.2 to 6.1 MB, ~30 to ~17 MB in memory). Draw calls were estimated at ~800 by day and ~1,200
  at night before; the cuts remove several hundred (estimate, not measured).
- **HUD (visual):** status line no longer covers the info lines and coins; info split into two
  short lines; rig line at the bottom centre; toasts low and fading; 64 px idle buttons that step
  aside in a fight; fight bars under the status; larger touch labels; CLUTCH- clears REEL; one
  theme for every button and panel; lit and shadowed rocks.
- **Retention and economy:** a sure first bite, 30 s wait cap, a 3-coin minimum per fish, no
  weight goal that needs a net you do not own, a growing day-on-the-water bonus that never punishes
  a missed day, Turkish names on the catch card and in the journal.

## Graphics pass (builds 67-72), checked on real screenshots

Godot 4.4 now runs in the cloud session under a virtual display (`tools/shots/shot.gd`), so these
were seen, not guessed (software rendering; colours and layout are right, fps is not the phone's).
- The top-down sea read as white static (per-pixel glints): now calm, broad, painted highlights.
- Painted, seamless ground tiles (sand, pebbles, rock, cobble, planks, concrete, grass) replace the
  flat fills on every shore, pier, quay, bridge and town.
- Soft cloud shadows and lamp light; nights lit by the real moon; lit and shadowed rocks; surf
  under the rocks; gulls with bodies and dark wing tips; tapering rods in the first-person view.
- Catch card moved top left beside the fish; map buttons can no longer be pushed off screen; the
  title screen shows the painted fish; Turkish names spelled properly everywhere.

## Still open, ranked

   panel scroll, fps on a rocky spot and at night, the hanging-fish swing.
2. **Turkish UI** (largest D1 risk per the retention review; the owner said not now).
3. **Step-by-step coaching** instead of the one text card (benchmark: Fishing Planet is criticised
   for its onboarding).
4. **"In season now / leaves this month" badges** and **bronze/silver/gold weight stars per
   species** in the journal (benchmark: Animal Crossing's monthly rotation, Fishing Clash
   collections).
5. **Performance, next step:** circles in `figure.gd` (people, cats) and the town's trees are drawn
   with one disc texture since build 66 (not measured); draw the static ground once; split the town's static drawing
   into chunks. Add draw-call and process-time numbers to the build label to measure on the phone.
6. **Economy:** a 40 kg grouper can pay ~2,870 coins, more than everything in the shop; small live
   bait fish give coins and also go in the bucket; the 30-coin tournament entry creates no tension.
7. **Painted ground tiles and character sprites** for the "too artificial" top-down view (needs art
   from the owner, see `docs/ART_BRIEF_AI.md`).
8. **iOS/Android:** only a Web export preset; no ETC2/ASTC; no icon or orientation settings; the
   project name is still "Global Fishing RPG".
9. **Code structure:** `shore_fishing.gd` is ~2,800 lines; split HUD, renderer and tournament.
10. **Analytics** (after validation, with consent): session_start, outing_start, bite_result,
    fight_end, catch, outing_end, goal_done, daily_challenge, purchase, level_up,
    local_turn_in, tournament_end.
